## Prompt handling notes

- Use Higgsfield/Seedance **First and Last Frames** mode for Reels 1, 2, 4, 6 and 7 as specified by Claude.
- Keep the generated problem frame first and the mapped Qobban render last.
- Select a vertical 9:16 output and use a fit/contain treatment for the 4:5 last frame when the interface offers it; avoid a crop that removes the product mechanism or feet.
- Never allow the video model to redesign a product. Add: `Match the last-frame product's proportions, mechanism, finish and colour exactly; preserve rigid geometry.`
- Keep people secondary and faces soft. The product/mechanism must remain the sharp focal point.
