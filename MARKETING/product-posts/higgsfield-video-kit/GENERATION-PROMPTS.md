# Starter-frame generation prompts

Built-in image generation was used. All frames were requested as vertical 9:16 photorealistic editorial photography in Bahrain, with warm pale limestone/cream plaster, beige paving or floor tile, straight verticals and natural Gulf light.

Global negative direction applied throughout: no generated copy, logos, watermarks, brand names, visible product badges, sparks, flames, smoke, lens flare, HDR halos, oversaturation, stock-photo staging, ornate scrollwork, distorted geometry, bent verticals, snow, lush lawns, Western suburban housing, or sharply focused faces.
