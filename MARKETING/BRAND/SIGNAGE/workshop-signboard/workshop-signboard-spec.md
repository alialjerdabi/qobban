# Qobban Fabrication Workshop Signboard

## Design intent

Long horizontal workshop fascia using the approved Qobban precision-led industrial system. The revised composition follows the client-supplied layout reference: a large logo zone at left, a dominant centered workshop identity, one compact horizontal service strip and a technical drawing/contact zone at right. The building photograph remains unchanged outside the installed sign area. The artwork uses a matte charcoal foundation, warm-white identity typography, construction-yellow accents, a restrained measurement grid, consistent technical service icons and a high-visibility phone panel.

## Working proportions and size

- Master artwork ratio: **4:1**
- Editable artboard: **6000 × 1500 px / SVG units**
- Nominal production size: **4000 × 1000 mm**
- Print raster export: **12000 × 3000 px** (approximately 76 ppi at nominal full size)
- Flat review export: **6000 × 1500 px**
- Web preview: **2000 × 500 px**
- Façade source photograph: **1600 × 1200 px**
- Mockup placement quadrilateral in the supplied photograph: approximately `(69,329)`, `(1395,363)`, `(1410,648)`, `(61,616)`

The nominal physical size is an assumption. Confirm the actual fascia width, height, mounting depth and edge conditions on site before fabrication.

## Typography

- Arabic: **Almarai ExtraBold / Bold / Regular**. This is the packaged Qobban Arabic family available in the project and the closest approved production typeface currently present.
- English: **Bahnschrift SemiBold / Regular**, with Arial Narrow as a fallback.
- Primary hierarchy: `ورشة قبّان`
- Secondary hierarchy: `حدادة وتصنيع`
- English support: `FABRICATION WORKSHOP`

All Arabic and English text remains editable in the SVG master. Outline fonts only in the final fabrication copy after text, spelling and dimensions are approved. Keep an unoutlined editable source.

## Logo usage

- Approved repository asset used without redrawing: `week-1-qobban-v2/assets/qobban-logo-lockup.png`
- Preserve original aspect ratio.
- Minimum clear space: **25% of the symbol height** on all sides.
- Do not place service icons, measurement lines or fabrication fasteners inside the clear-space zone.
- The current repository logo is a raster PNG. Obtain the approved vector logo (`AI`, `EPS`, `SVG` or vector PDF) before final CNC routing or large-format cut-letter production.

## Service icon system

- Six consistently sized technical line icons.
- Warm-white primary stroke with one restrained yellow detail.
- Nominal master stroke: **12 SVG units**.
- Equal compact service modules separated by restrained vertical rules.
- Arabic service labels and uppercase English descriptors are paired horizontally with their icons for improved distance readability.
- Avoid replacing individual icons with stock icons from unrelated families.

## Recommended production construction

1. **4 mm exterior-grade aluminium composite panels**, matte charcoal face.
2. Panels fixed to a level galvanized or powder-coated subframe; typical preliminary frame section **40 × 20 mm** or as determined by the fabricator.
3. Logo and main Arabic heading produced as precision-cut acrylic or aluminium dimensional lettering.
4. Yellow phone panel produced as a separate yellow ACP/acrylic insert or premium exterior vinyl applied to a prepared panel.
5. Thin yellow lower strip fabricated as a continuous separate strip or accurately wrapped exterior vinyl.
6. All seams aligned with the artwork grid and kept away from the logo and large headings where possible.
7. Exterior-grade UV-stable print, paint and adhesive system suitable for Bahrain heat and sun exposure.

Structural support, wind loading, electrical routing, waterproofing and mounting details must be confirmed by the appointed fabricator/site engineer.

## Illumination recommendation

- Preferred: restrained **4000 K concealed halo illumination** behind the Qobban symbol and main Arabic heading only.
- Use 15–25 mm dimensional letters with a consistent stand-off and low-output sealed exterior LED modules.
- Keep service labels, technical drawing and measurement lines non-illuminated.
- Phone number may use subtle internally lit or externally washed illumination if night visibility is required.
- Avoid cool-blue LEDs, exposed LED dots, animated lighting, strong edge glow or full-panel lightbox treatment.

## Mockup and installation assumptions

- The digital mockup covers the workshop frontage with one continuous fascia, following the width and proportions shown in the client reference.
- The workshop structure, roof, posts, openings, materials and surrounding neighboring signage remain unchanged.
- Perspective is matched to the visible fascia in the supplied photo.
- Final site installation requires verified dimensions, level datum, obstruction checks, electrical access and approval of the neighboring sign boundaries.
