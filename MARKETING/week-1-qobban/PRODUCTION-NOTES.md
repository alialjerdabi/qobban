## Missing real-photo categories

- Gates
- Pergolas / shade structures
- Railings / staircases
- Canopies / car shades
- Maintenance / repair / recoating
- Installation / handover sequence

## Recommended client replacements

Prioritize one clean, straight-on or three-quarter finished-project image for each missing category. Capture full product geometry, hardware/fixings, relationship to the property, and at least one craftsmanship close-up. Avoid vehicles, temporary construction material, exposed cables, and identifiable client information.

## Accuracy note

Arabic copy was supplied verbatim in the generation prompts. Because raster image generation can introduce letterform or punctuation variance, the final files should receive a native-speaker proofread before paid publication. The approved logo reference was supplied to every designed frame; compare the rendered lockup against the master vector before production release.
