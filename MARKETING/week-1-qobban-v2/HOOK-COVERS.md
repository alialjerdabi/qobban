# Qobban Carousel Hook Covers

The two carousel covers were redesigned specifically for thumbnail scanning in profile and discovery grids.

## Service carousel

- Primary hook: `شنو نقدر ننفذ لك؟`
- Almarai ExtraBold at large scale inside the central crop-safe zone.
- One short supporting line only.
- No contact bar, feature list, or dense service labels.

## Contractor checklist carousel

- Primary hook: `لا تختار على السعر بس`
- A large yellow `5` creates immediate numerical recognition.
- Supporting line explains the value of swiping without competing with the headline.

## Shared rules

- 1080 × 1350 px.
- Almarai ExtraBold headlines.
- High-contrast charcoal, warm white, and Qobban yellow.
- Key text remains inside the central square-safe region.
- Small logo and slide count remain secondary.
- Previous V2 covers are preserved under `previous-covers`.
