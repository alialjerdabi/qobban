# Qobban Week 1 — Production Notes V2

## What changed

- Rebuilt all 16 finished designs using one locked campaign template derived from the supplied gas-enclosure reference.
- Removed alternating panel positions and slide-specific layout experimentation.
- Standardized every frame to the same 620 px photography zone, 460 px charcoal panel, logo position, coordinates, slide counter, headline width, three-row feature system, technical rulers, contact bar, and footer.
- Arabic copy is now rendered deterministically rather than generated as part of the raster scene.
- Typography correction: the initial V2 fallback families were removed. All Arabic text is now embedded and rendered with Almarai, matching the uploaded post reference: ExtraBold headlines, Light support copy, Regular feature labels, and Bold CTAs.
- Replaced text abbreviations in the contact bar with consistent Instagram and phone/WhatsApp-style symbols.

## Delivered

- 16 final Instagram 4:5 designs at 1080 × 1350 px.
- 3 retained no-text base images.
- One master contact sheet.
- Shared ImageGPT-generated charcoal panel texture.
- Reusable layout renderer for future Qobban campaigns.
