# Qobban Brand Introduction — Creative Directions

These concepts introduce Qobban through its brand principles rather than through products or service categories. All three avoid the prior split-panel advertising template.

## 01 — The Precision Field

A monumental spatial interpretation of the Qobban Q-frame and spirit level. Alignment rays converge on the yellow center, turning the brand mark into a calm architectural experience.

Copy: `الدقة معيارنا` / `PRECISION IS OUR STANDARD.`

## 02 — Bahrain Light / Measured Shadow

Hard Bahraini daylight projects the Qobban geometry as a measured shadow across warm limestone. The concept communicates that precision becomes visible in the result.

Copy: `الدقة تبين في النتيجة` / `الدقة معيارنا`.

## 03 — Alignment Portal

Repeating charcoal frames converge on one yellow horizon and one centered level bubble. The brand is expressed as a system where every decision begins with correct alignment.

Copy: `كل شيء يبدأ من قياس صحيح` / `الدقة معيارنا`.
