# Social Media Prompts

Image and video prompts for the Instagram + TikTok launch. Style and negative
blocks are owned by [Image_Generation_Rules.md](Image_Generation_Rules.md) —
prepend and append them to every prompt below. Website slots are separate:
[../site/IMAGE_PROMPTS.md](../site/IMAGE_PROMPTS.md).

**Naming:** `SOC-01`… (stills), `VID-01`… (video).
**Ratios:** feed 4:5, stories/reels/TikTok 9:16.

Headline text is set in design over these images, never generated into them.
Every prompt names which area to keep quiet for the overlay.

## Launch set — the first seven posts

Matches the priority order in the marketing plan.

**SOC-01 · Brand intro (feed 4:5)** — overlay: "Precision is our standard."
> A single matte charcoal aluminium villa gate slat detail, photographed dead-on
> so the horizontal lines run perfectly level across the frame. Hard side light
> from the left. Lower two-thirds of the frame in deep shadow and empty for text.

**SOC-02 · Brand montage cover (9:16)** — reel cover, overlay: "Built With Precision"
> Vertical composition of a completed contemporary Bahraini villa entrance at
> dusk, dark charcoal gate centred, boundary wall receding on both sides. Warm
> light spilling from beyond the gate. Top third open sky for headline text.

**SOC-03 · Service stack cover (feed 4:5)** — overlay: "Gates. Pergolas. Railings."
> A tight architectural composition where three distinct metal elements are
> visible in one frame — a gate edge in the foreground, a pergola blade line
> mid-ground, a balcony railing beyond. Layered depth, hard afternoon light,
> matte charcoal throughout. Right half quieter for text.

**SOC-04 · Gates lead (feed 4:5)** — overlay: "Your gate is the first impression."
> A wide aluminium sliding gate on a Bahraini villa photographed straight on from
> across the street, perfectly symmetrical, late golden light raking across the
> slats. Pale limestone boundary wall either side. Generous sky above for text.

**SOC-05 · Gate before/after (feed 4:5)** — overlay: "Before / After"
> Split frame, identical camera position and light on both halves. Left: an old
> rusting painted steel gate, coating blistered, sagging on its hinges. Right: the
> same opening with a clean matte charcoal aluminium slat gate installed. Hard
> vertical division down the exact centre.

**SOC-06 · Buyer checklist cover (feed 4:5)** — overlay: "Before you approve that quote"
> Overhead flat lay on a dark charcoal surface: a folded printed specification
> document, two small metal finish swatches, a steel rule and a pencil, arranged
> on a strict grid with generous spacing. Even soft overhead light. No readable
> text on the document. Upper half empty for headline.

**SOC-07 · Pergola lead (feed 4:5)** — overlay: "Shade that adds value."
> An aluminium louvred pergola over a villa terrace at golden hour, viewed from
> inside the shade looking out toward bright sun. Striped shadows across pale
> stone paving, outdoor seating at the edge of frame. Left third in deep shade
> for text.

## Content pillar stills

Reusable across the calendar. Generate 2–3 variants of each.

**SOC-08 · Precision proof (feed 4:5)**
> Extreme macro of a spirit level laid against a freshly installed matte charcoal
> metal edge, the yellow vial and centred bubble sharp in the foreground, the
> metal running away into shallow focus. Dark background, hard raking light.

**SOC-09 · Climate durability (feed 4:5)**
> Two metal sample panels side by side against a dark charcoal background: one
> showing corroded, blistered failed coating, the other a flawless matte charcoal
> powder-coated surface. Clinical, evenly lit, product-photography precision.

**SOC-10 · Architectural effect (feed 4:5)**
> Wide architectural photograph of a contemporary Bahraini villa frontage where a
> dark metal gate visually aligns with the horizontal coursing of the boundary
> wall and the building's window line. Flat even morning light, straight-on, no
> perspective distortion. Calm and considered.

**SOC-11 · Craft detail (feed 4:5)**
> Macro of a corner joint on matte charcoal metalwork, weld dressed completely
> flat so the seam is nearly invisible, edge crisp. Shallow depth of field, hard
> directional light revealing the surface texture.

**SOC-12 · Workshop credibility (feed 4:5)**
> Interior of a clean, organised metal fabrication workshop: aluminium and steel
> stock racked vertically, a large steel fabrication table, cool daylight from
> high windows, spotless concrete floor. Calm and competent. No sparks, no people.

**SOC-13 · Site professionalism (9:16)**
> Vertical documentary shot of a villa installation in progress: finished floor
> protected with clean sheeting, tools laid out in order on a mat, a charcoal gate
> leaf standing ready. Tidy and controlled, hard daylight. No faces.

**SOC-14 · Material education (feed 4:5)**
> A row of five metal finish samples fanned on a dark charcoal surface — matte
> black, anodised aluminium, timber-effect, natural mill, warm grey — evenly lit
> from above, sharp across all five. Catalogue-precise, generous margins.

## Video prompts

Same style and negative blocks. Vertical 9:16 unless stated. Keep cuts composed —
fast is fine, frantic is not.

**VID-01 · Brand montage · 20–25s**
> Sequence, hard cuts: (1) a tape measure pulled across a villa driveway opening,
> (2) a gate frame clamped square in a workshop jig, (3) a matte charcoal profile
> emerging from powder coating, (4) a gate leaf lifted into position on site,
> (5) the finished gate closing smoothly at dusk. Consistent dark charcoal palette,
> hard natural light throughout, no sparks or flames. Steady controlled camera.

**VID-02 · Gate before/after · 15–20s**
> Locked-off camera on a Bahraini villa entrance. Old rusted gate in frame, then a
> hard cut to the same framing with a new matte charcoal slat gate installed and
> opening. Identical position, identical light. Final cut pulls wider to show the
> full frontage.

**VID-03 · Pergola showcase · 15–20s**
> Slow push-in beneath an aluminium louvred pergola, blades rotating and the
> striped shadow pattern sweeping across pale stone paving below, ending on
> outdoor seating fully in shade. Golden hour, warm and calm.

**VID-04 · Precision close-ups · 10–15s**
> Rapid series of macro shots: a level bubble settling to centre, a weld seam
> dressed flat, a slat edge in raking light, a hinge closing with no play, a
> gloved hand wiping a finished surface. Percussive rhythm, no voiceover needed.

**VID-05 · Site professionalism · 15–20s**
> Documentary sequence on a villa site: protective sheeting laid, a laser level
> projecting a line across a wall, a post set and checked plumb, tools returned
> to a mat, site swept at the end. No faces, natural sound, unhurried.

**VID-06 · Material and finish explainer · 20–25s**
> Studio sequence on a dark charcoal surface: finish sample panels placed one by
> one into frame from above, then a cut to the same finish installed outdoors in
> hard sun. Clean, instructional, product-photography lighting.

## Platform notes

Instagram and TikTok take the same source footage but not the same edit. TikTok
rewards a faster opening and on-screen text within the first second; Instagram
tolerates a slower, more composed build. Cut two versions from one shoot rather
than cross-posting a single export.

Bahrain's Instagram audience skews 59.6% male, and TikTok out-reaches Instagram
among adults — see [../RESEARCH/Bahrain_Market.md](../RESEARCH/Bahrain_Market.md).
Weight the gate and frontage material toward Instagram; the process and
before/after material travels further on TikTok.
