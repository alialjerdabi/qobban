# Project Overview

## What Qobban is

Qobban (قبان) is a Bahrain architectural metalwork business — gates, pergolas,
railings, canopies and custom fabrication for villas, commercial and industrial
properties. Brand line: **Precision is our standard.**

## What this repository is

The complete brand, business and product documentation set for Qobban, plus the
specification for its website. It is a knowledge base, not an application —
markdown only, no build.

## Conventions

- One fact, one file. Link, never duplicate. See [.claude/rules/documentation.md](.claude/rules/documentation.md).
- Terms are defined once, in [11_GLOSSARY.md](11_GLOSSARY.md).
- `[CONFIRM]` marks a claim awaiting client verification. Do not present a
  `[CONFIRM]` item as fact in customer-facing work.
