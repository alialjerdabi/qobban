# Competitor Research

Bahrain architectural metalwork competitors. Researched 2026-07-27.
Market conditions: [Bahrain_Market.md](Bahrain_Market.md).

## The market splits in two

**Established general fabricators** — capable, website-led, socially near-absent.

**Instagram-native specialists** — present on platform, single-category, generic
branding: @bahrain.pergola, @bahrain_pergola_design, pergolabahrain.com,
Rolling Shutters Bahrain, Sedar Bahrain (free measurement + installation).

## What the strong ones do well

- Present broad capability and real fabrication scale.
- Make the climate problem visible — corrosion resistance, powder coating,
  anti-rust treatment stated up front.
- Sell through WhatsApp/DM with fast, low-friction response.
- Offer free quotation, free measurement, and consultation as standard.

## Where they are weak

Category copy is interchangeable: "Professional Metal Fabrication in Bahrain",
"Premium Solutions", "Serving all over Bahrain", checkmark service lists. Brands
are distinguishable by name only.

The general fabricators have capability but no brand or visible craft. The
Instagram specialists have presence but no range and no design identity.

## The open lane

**No Bahrain competitor combines a designed brand identity, visible proof of
craft process, and the full architectural metalwork range.** That position is
unoccupied and is where Qobban should compete. See [../04_POSITIONING.md](../04_POSITIONING.md).

Corollary: the competitive fight on social is *category-level*, not brand-level.
Qobban is not out-executing strong incumbents — it is entering an empty room.

## Differentiation that actually bites

"Free consultation" is not a wedge; the market already gives it away. The
defensible wedge is a **written, priced scope naming material specification,
coating system, and warranty period** — which almost nobody in this market
issues. It attacks the reliability objection instead of the price-of-a-quote
objection.

## Limits of this research

Competitor Instagram follower counts, engagement and post performance could not
be retrieved — profile data is behind a login wall. Statements about competitor
social *performance* are inference from public/indexed content, not measurement.
Directory supplier counts cited in earlier internal reports are unverified.
