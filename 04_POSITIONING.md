## Statement

**Qobban is Bahrain's precision-led architectural metalwork brand — gates,
pergolas, railings and custom fabrication, engineered to measure, finished for
the climate, and installed to belong.**

Positioning line: **Built with precision. Finished for Bahrain. Designed to belong.**
Brand line (existing): **Precision is our standard.**

## Frame of reference

Not a welding workshop. Not a shading supplier. An architectural metalwork
partner — closer to a fabrication studio than a trade contractor.

This matters commercially: it moves the comparison away from price-per-metre and
toward specification, finish and delivery quality.

## Three defensible territories

**1. Precision proof.** Measurement, level checks, alignment, weld cleanliness,
edge finishing, fit against façade lines. Turns the tagline into evidence.
Competitors claim quality; Qobban shows tolerance.

**2. Bahrain-ready durability.** Material choice, coating system, and what
actually survives heat, humidity and coastal air. Framed architecturally and
practically — not as a chemicals lecture.

**3. Architectural effect.** A gate is a first impression. A railing shapes how a
staircase feels. A pergola creates usable outdoor living. Competitors sell the
product; Qobban sells the outcome.

## Proof obligations

A positioning claim the business cannot deliver is a liability. To hold this
position Qobban must consistently issue:

- A written, priced scope naming material spec, coating system and warranty period
- Documented measurement before fabrication
- A clean-site installation standard
- Photographic handover

These are the differentiator. See [RESEARCH/Competitor_Research.md](RESEARCH/Competitor_Research.md).

## What Qobban does not claim

- Not the cheapest. Price-led buyers are not the target.
- Not the largest. Shafiq Glass and Sabro have more capacity.
- Not everything-for-everyone. Industrial fabrication supports credibility but
  does not lead the brand.

## Positioning against the field

| Competitor type | Their strength | Qobban's counter |
|---|---|---|
| Large fabricators (Shafiq, Sabro, Neptune) | Scale, capability, history | Brand, design intent, visible process |
| Residential specialists (DAK, Al Reda) | Local reach, established trust | Precision proof, written specification |
| Pergola-only Instagram brands | Platform presence, category focus | Full range, architectural credibility |
