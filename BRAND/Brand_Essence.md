## Idea

**Precision made visible.**

The logo is the brand argument: the letter Q built as a frame, with a spirit
level set inside it. Measurement is not a step in the work — it is the identity.
Everything the brand produces should look like it was set level before it was
released.

## Name

قبان / Qobban — the steelyard balance, a traditional weighing instrument.
Weighing means judgement, accuracy and trust. The bilingual lockup is a
statement of place: Bahraini roots, international standard.

## Promise

Precision is our standard.

Read as a commitment with consequences: measured before fabrication, specified
before quoting, level at installation, documented at handover.

## Personality

Exact · Composed · Understated · Grounded

Qobban is the calm professional in a category that shouts. It does not use
superlatives, exclamation marks, or urgency tactics. Confidence is shown by how
little it needs to claim.

## Attributes

| Attribute | Meaning | How it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Precision | Measured, aligned, tolerant to spec | Close-up detail, level checks, dimension callouts |
| Durability | Built for heat, humidity, coastal air | Named coating systems, material logic, warranty |
| Restraint | Nothing decorative without reason | Dark fields, one accent, generous space |
| Belonging | Work that fits its architecture | Wide shots showing façade harmony |

## Brand in one paragraph

Qobban makes architectural metalwork for Bahrain — gates, pergolas, railings and
custom fabrication. The work is measured before it is made, specified before it
is priced, and finished for a climate that punishes shortcuts. The name means a
balance; the mark is a level. Both say the same thing: it is right, or it is
not finished.

## What the brand is not

Not industrial-macho. No sparks, flames, smoke overlays or heroic welder imagery.
Not luxury-decorative. No gold, no ornament, no aspirational lifestyle staging.
Not budget. Never leads with price or discount.
