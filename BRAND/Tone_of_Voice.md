# Tone of Voice

Personality source: [Brand_Essence.md](Brand_Essence.md). Claims and lines: [Messaging.md](Messaging.md).

## Principle

Write the way precise work looks: measured, specific, unhurried, and free of
decoration. The category shouts. Qobban does not need to.

## Four rules

**1. Specific over superlative.** Never "best quality" or "premium solutions".
Name the material, the coating, the tolerance, the timeline.
> Not: *Premium quality gates built to the highest standards.*
> Yes: *Powder-coated aluminium gate, measured on site, installed in one day.*

**2. Say the useful thing first.** No warm-up. Lead with the answer, the price
logic, or the risk the reader is actually worried about.

**3. Short declaratives.** Full stops over commas. Most sentences under 18 words.
The rhythm should feel settled, not breathless.

**4. Honest about limits.** Say what a material will not do, what a timeline
depends on, what is out of scope. Admitting a constraint buys more trust than
claiming perfection — and it is the reliability objection answered directly.

## Never

Exclamation marks · emoji in body copy · "Looking for...?" openers · checkmark
service lists · "we pride ourselves" · manufactured urgency · discount language ·
ALL CAPS for emphasis in sentences · "solutions" as a filler noun.

## Register by audience

| Audience | Adjustment |
|---|---|
| Villa owners | Plain language. Explain material choices in outcomes — heat, rust, maintenance, appearance. No jargon without a translation. |
| Architects and designers | Technical register is welcome. Tolerances, finishes, fixing details, drawing references. Assume competence. |
| Contractors and developers | Schedule, capacity, sequencing, repeatability. Brief and operational. |
| Commercial and industrial | Compliance, safety, load, durability, documentation. |

## Calls to action

Direct and low-pressure. The next step should feel like a conversation, not a
conversion.

Good: *Send your dimensions and we'll spec it.* · *Book a site measurement.* ·
*Tell us the space and we'll tell you what it needs.*
Bad: *Get your FREE quote now!!* · *Don't miss out.* · *Limited time offer.*

## Bilingual note

Arabic copy is a rewrite, not a translation. Keep the same restraint and
specificity; do not carry English sentence structure across. `[CONFIRM]` Arabic
copywriter before Arabic content begins.

## Test

If a competitor could paste the sentence onto their own site unchanged, it is
not Qobban's sentence. Rewrite it with something only Qobban would know.
