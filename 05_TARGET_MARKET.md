# Target Market

Segment detail lives in [CUSTOMERS/](CUSTOMERS/). This file sets priority only.

## Priority segments

**1. Villa owners (primary).** Bahraini and expatriate homeowners upgrading
frontage, outdoor space or interiors. Buy gates, pergolas, railings, canopies.
Decide on appearance, durability and trust. Reachable on Instagram — which skews
59.6% male in Bahrain, matching the frontage/gate decision-maker.
→ [CUSTOMERS/Villa_Owners.md](CUSTOMERS/Villa_Owners.md)

**2. Architects and interior designers (multiplier).** Low volume, high value,
repeat. Specify rather than buy. Judge on tolerance, finish quality, drawing
literacy and reliability — not price. Winning one wins many projects.
→ [CUSTOMERS/Architects_and_Designers.md](CUSTOMERS/Architects_and_Designers.md)

**3. Contractors and developers (volume).** New-build handovers across the
7,000+ unit pipeline. Buy on schedule reliability and consistency at repeat
scale. Margin is thinner; cash flow is steadier.
→ [CUSTOMERS/Contractors.md](CUSTOMERS/Contractors.md) · [CUSTOMERS/Developers.md](CUSTOMERS/Developers.md)

**4. Commercial and industrial (credibility).** Offices, retail, facilities,
plants. Supports the capability story and project-value signalling. Should not
lead consumer-facing marketing.
→ [CUSTOMERS/Commercial_Clients.md](CUSTOMERS/Commercial_Clients.md) · [CUSTOMERS/Industrial_Clients.md](CUSTOMERS/Industrial_Clients.md)

## Geography

Bahrain-wide, with concentration where villa stock and new handovers cluster:
Riffa, Saar, Janabiya, Hamala, Budaiya, Amwaj, Diyar Al Muharraq, Durrat, and
the new districts (Salman City, East Sitra, East Hidd, Madinat Khalifa).

## Who Qobban is not for

Lowest-price buyers, single-item repair-only enquiries with no upgrade path, and
projects where no site access or measurement is permitted before fabrication.
Declining these protects the positioning in [04_POSITIONING.md](04_POSITIONING.md).

## Buying triggers

New villa handover · resale or rental preparation · rust or coating failure on
existing metalwork · summer approaching (shade) · security concern · renovation
or extension · façade or landscaping work already underway.
→ [CUSTOMERS/Buying_Triggers.md](CUSTOMERS/Buying_Triggers.md)
